Hermes ERP

One system for the whole operation of Grupo Hermes, from the shelf to the cash register

Grupo Hermes sells through a physical store, online, on WhatsApp and on marketplaces. Each channel had its own notes, and stock, cash and bills lived somewhere else.

Hermes brings it together: products with cost and margin, sales from every channel checked against real stock, cash sessions, payables and receivables, reports you can export, and simplified invoices for internal control.

Hermes is in beta. The Grupo Hermes team is testing it in the daily routine before the official launch, and every round of feedback goes straight into the next version.

01The challenge

A sale on WhatsApp could sell an item the store had already sold. Margins were calculated by hand. Closing the cash meant adding up notes at the end of the day.

The team didn’t need a giant ERP with a hundred screens. They needed the five screens they use all day to be fast, correct and impossible to get wrong.

02What we built

Rules where mistakes used to happen. A sale only goes through if there is stock and the cash session is open. Sale price comes from cost and margin, with a live preview.

One place for the money. Cash sessions with calculated balance, bills that change status by themselves on the due date, and a statement of everything that came in and out.

Reports people actually open. Sales, stock, margins and the year at a glance, exported to XLSX or printed to PDF.
