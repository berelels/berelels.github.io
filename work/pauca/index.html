Pauca

A home screen that doesn’t ask for your attention all the time

Pauca is an Android launcher made of text. Your apps live in cards, with the names you choose, and a focus mode silences the phone for real instead of hiding a badge.

It’s live on the Play Store. Pauca is a fork of Olauncher and stays open source under GPLv3.

01The idea

Phones are designed to be opened. Pauca is designed to be closed: you find the app you came for, by its name, and nothing else pulls you in.

02What we built

Cards and names. Group apps the way you think about them and rename them to what they are for.

Focus mode. One switch that cuts the noise, with the start time on screen so you know how long you’ve held it.

Styles. Paper, dark, wallpaper, mono and zen, so the phone looks like something you chose.
