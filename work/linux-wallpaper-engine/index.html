Linux Wallpaper Engine

Animated wallpapers from the Steam Workshop, on Linux, without Windows in the middle

A script that brings Steam Workshop wallpapers to GNOME with X11, running natively. It works on Zorin OS, Ubuntu and the distros built on them.

It started as a personal itch and is now public for anyone on GNOME who wants a living desktop. Open source under MIT.
