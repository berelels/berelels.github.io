Founder, developer, the one who replies to your email

Catta Studios is an independent software studio in Divinópolis, Minas Gerais, Brasil. It started in automation and Linux administration and became a place to build apps and systems that real people use at work.

Behind it is Gabriel Dias, an IT technician trained at Senac Minas, with the habit of only shipping what he would use himself. The work covers MVC architecture, local and cloud databases, PDF reports, REST APIs, and interfaces in Python, React and TypeScript, plus complete web systems with JavaScript, PHP and PostgreSQL.

Being small is the point. You get one person who knows your project end to end, who answers you directly and who is still around after launch.
