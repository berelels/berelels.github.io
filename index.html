Our approach

No surprises is a feature

Most software projects go wrong in the same places: vague scope, silence for weeks, and a handover that never happens. The process is built to remove each one.

01

Talk

A free 30 minute call. You explain the problem, we ask the boring questions that save money later.

02

Proposal

Scope, price, dates and what’s not included, in writing. You sign only when it reads right.

03

Build

Every week a link to the real thing. You click around, comment, and we adjust while it’s cheap.

04

Launch

It goes live, you get the code, the docs and the keys, and support stays on for 14 days.
